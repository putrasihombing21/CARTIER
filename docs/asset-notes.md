# Prototype asset notes

All three campaign assets were generated for this project with the built-in image generation tool, one generation per image. Models are fictional adults. These are concept garments, not photographs of released products. No supplied or existing fashion-brand logos, celebrity photography, album art, crosses or monograms were used.

- `public/assets/world.webp`: two fictional adult models in oversized black garments; concrete backstage; hard flash; grayscale analog grain. Generated at 1254 × 1254, compressed to WebP.
- `public/assets/campaign.webp`: industrial loading dock at night; a long black coat; a blurred walking figure; corrugated metal and monochrome flash. Generated at 1672 × 941, compressed to WebP.
- `public/assets/garment-studies.webp`: equal triptych of a washed black tee, boxy zip jacket, and hooded long coat; invisible mannequins and charcoal backgrounds. Generated at 2172 × 724, compressed to WebP.

The wordmark is original uppercase geometric lettering drawn as editable SVG paths. The emblem is an original incomplete angular C ring with one detached metal fragment. Both are temporary design studies, not finalized or registered identities.

Licensed typefaces: Bebas Neue, Barlow Condensed, and IBM Plex Mono, provided by their respective `@fontsource` packages with license files. The interface uses Lucide icons. No existing luxury-brand script lettering is used.
